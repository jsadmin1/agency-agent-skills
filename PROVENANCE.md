# Provenance and verification

This collection contains original skills by jsadmin1 and reviewed generic
derivatives of that author's local workflows. The original material is MIT
licensed. Third-party tool names describe integrations; those tools, accounts
and services have their own licenses and terms and are not included here.

The exact per-file hashes are in [skills-manifest.json](skills-manifest.json).
No private source inventory, account value, client record, machine-specific
conversation identity or personal transcript is distributed.

`make-no-mistakes` is copied with its existing MIT license from
https://github.com/jsadmin1/make-no-mistakes at commit
`614d87fb944bfb036b3ff146303c2a4ce9264fb2`. Its original repository is unchanged.
Its model-call evaluation runner is not part of this distribution.

Other original skills are exported separately from private live installations.
Do not assume that installing one configures its third-party tools or initializes
a project. Follow each skill's prerequisites and verify actual runtime state.

YouTube related-video rule applies only when the approved workflow requires a

final corrections received maintainer checks, not a third independent review.

Third-party vendor skill bundles and commercial copywriting material are not
redistributed here. Instructions may reference external tools and public
documentation; those references do not relicense or bundle the tools themselves.
